Chocolate Cake

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Maida
  • 1/2 cup Sugar
  • 1/2 cup Cocoa powder
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1/2 cup Milk
  • Chocolate for topping

Steps

  1. Mix maida, sugar and cocoa powder
  2. Add eggs and milk and mix well
  3. Pour in cake tin
  4. Bake for 30 mins at 180 degree
  5. Add chocolate topping and serve
